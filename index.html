The visualisations below are drawn from a policy brief on Haryana's Energy Sector produced for the Haryana State Pollution Control Board (HSPCB), May 2024. The analysis covers installed capacity, renewable energy deployment, electricity access disparities, and distribution losses.

Tracking Haryana's Renewable Energy Trajectory

A decade-long view of cumulative renewable energy installation in Haryana, disaggregated by solar, bioenergy, and small hydropower. The overlay line captures total capacity growth, highlighting a 24.8% CAGR since 2014–15 and solar's rapid rise to dominance from 2017–18.
